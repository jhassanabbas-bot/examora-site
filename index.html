Features
Everything you need to prepare
📄
PDF & Word support
Upload PDFs (including scanned-text documents like AAPM TG reports) or Microsoft Word .docx files. Both work seamlessly.
📚
Book Mode
For textbooks, Examora auto-detects chapters from the PDF table of contents. Study one chapter at a time with bookmarks so you resume where you left off.
🔍
Adaptive summarisation
Short reports get a quick single summary. Long documents (150+ pages) are split into sections and summarised comprehensively — nothing gets missed.
🎚️
Difficulty levels
Easy, Medium, or Hard. Exam mode holds feedback until submission. Study mode gives instant answer and explanation after each question.
⚑
Flag & navigate
Flag questions for review, jump between questions via the grid, and come back to flagged items before submitting.
📊
Results & download
Score banner, per-question review with explanations, and a downloadable CSV so you can track your performance over time.
